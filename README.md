# 48mm Custom Cast Wheel Mold | FTC DECODE (2025–2026)

A specialized multi-piece wheel mold designed by **FTC Team 21982 Inkineers** for casting high-performance, custom-durometer intake and compliance wheels using **Alumilite Amazing Mold Maker**. 

Engineered specifically for the dynamic game element interaction requirements of the **FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) DECODE 2025–2026 season**.

| Parameter | Specification |

## Design Features

* **High-Grip Tread Geometry:** Features an aggressive scalloped/scallop-ridged outer perimeter designed to maximize surface contact and friction against game elements.
* **Tuned Compliance Spoke Web:** Curved internal 4-spoke structural web allows local deformation under compression, absorbing impact while maintaining axial driving torque.
* **Precision Mold Retention:** Integrated alignment tabs and perimeter bolt bosses ensure tight seal compression, minimizing resin flash and seam lines during curing.
* **Very Bouncy:** Very fun to throw around.

## Molding Instructions

1. 3D print a TPU wheel frame for the mold  8% infill and Honeycomb pattern (Recommended: Cylinder modifier at the core + base of spokes, infill density 100%)
2. Assemble the mold frame with M4 Screws & align the TPU Wheel to fit

We recommend that you watch a video tutorial about the product before pouring the mold. Here was our process:
3. Create a mixture of ~5-6mm of each of the two solutions from Alumilite Amazing Mold Maker and mix thoroughly like beating an egg for 30-45 seconds (it solidifies fast...)
4. Pour evenly. The edges are the only places that have the mold.
5. Vibrate the mold so to release any air pockets & bubbles (we used a motor)
6. Wait 30-40 minutes for the mold to cure.
7. Remove excess imperfections on mold.
8. Become molding factory.

Note: When removing the mold, disassemble the mold frame first and take the wheel out with the side ring from the base.
